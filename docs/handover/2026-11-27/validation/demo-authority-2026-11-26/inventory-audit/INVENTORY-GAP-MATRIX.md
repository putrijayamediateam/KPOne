Runtime facts materially different from a palette-only reading:

- `Receiving`, `Transfers`, `StockCount`, `WriteOff`, `BatchHolds` resolve to `Stock` with selected tabs; `StockMovement`, `StockValue`, `MedicineSales` resolve to `Reports`. See wrapper lines252–257, not the older standalone layouts.
- `Stock` has group pills, secondary tabs/counts, selection-linked summary cards, search/filter toolbar, expandable rows, contextual actions and a collapsible helpful-info side rail. `My branch` / `HQ` changes the workspace. The current app instead has three primary stock tabs, grouped task links and a large Operations panel, with most workflows on separate pages.
- `InvReceive`, `InvDispatch`, `InvReceiveTransfer`, `InvReportDiff`, `InvPoApprove`, `InvRfq` open focused overlays above contextual Stock content. They are not simply links to arbitrary new full pages.
- Wrapper `NOTES` line244 explicitly excludes `InvLanes`, `InvMyWork`, `InvTimeline`, `InventoryFlow` as design notes. Do not turn them into production routes just because those HTML assets exist.
- Report controls have separate view/export permissions (wrapper220–227). A single Reports shell must not preload money/patient/branch data for tabs the actor cannot access.
- The wrapper and mock text are not the permission catalogue or accounting authority. Its samples still say cost policy unconfirmed and sometimes Director-only, show fixed names/locations/counts, permit simplified transitions and include proposed RFQ/Thursday/paid markers. Preserve newer owner-approved rules and real service capabilities.
