# 03 Consent procedure (DRAFT)

Written for the front desk (CA) and doctors. Keep it to one page when printed.
**[CONFIRM]** with the PDPC guideline whether treatment itself needs explicit consent beyond the notice, and
whether any step below is stricter or looser than the Act requires for sensitive (health) data.

## Principles

- Show or hand over the **privacy notice** (02) before collecting details. A poster at the counter plus a copy on request.
- Collect only what the form asks for. Do not ask for extra identity details "just in case".
- The tick in KPOne is **the clinic's record** that the conversation happened. It is not a signature.
- If a person refuses to give required identity details, explain the consequence (we may be unable to register
  or treat them) and let a supervisor decide. Do not pressure.

## Adult patient at the counter

1. Greet and say: "We keep your details to register and treat you. Our privacy notice is here. Do you agree?"
2. Register in KPOne.
3. Do not read out or show another patient's details.

## Patient registering by QR

1. The form shows the notice and a consent tick ("Saya bersetuju ..."). The patient must tick to submit.
2. A CA reviews before accepting. Unaccepted forms are deleted after 30 days automatically.
3. If the patient asks for help, a CA may fill the form with them but must not tick on their behalf without asking.

## Patient under 18

1. The parent or guardian provides details and agrees for the child. Record the guardian's name, relationship and phone (KPOne requires it for a known minor).
2. Check the guardian's identity if the clinic's rules require **[DECIDE]**.
3. A teenager attending alone: follow the doctor's judgement and the clinic's policy **[DECIDE; CONFIRM with MMC guidance]**.
4. The guardian's number is never used for WhatsApp links in KPOne.

## Patient with no known date of birth

The CA records an explicit adult confirmation (KPOne requires it). This is not verified age.

## Medical certificates, referral letters, attendance confirmations

1. The doctor asks the patient verbally before issuing a letter that names a recipient (an employer, a specialist): "This letter will be given to [recipient]. Is that OK?"
2. The doctor ticks the acknowledgement box in KPOne. For an MC that includes a reason, the patient's consent to print the reason is required.
3. The signed paper copy is kept by the clinic for **[period; owner set 1 year, which may be too short. CONFIRM, see 04]**.

## Panel and insurance patients

1. Tell the patient their claim details (name, member reference, visit date, invoice items) go to the Panel/TPA for payment.
2. Do not send clinical notes or diagnoses to a Panel unless the patient agrees and the Panel contract requires it.

## WhatsApp click-to-chat

The CA taps the WhatsApp icon to open a chat with a patient's saved number. Only do this for a reason related to
their care or visit. Do not put clinical detail in the message. KPOne sends nothing itself.

## Photos, videos and the waiting-room TV

1. The TV shows queue numbers by default. If a branch switches to names, put a sign on the wall and offer "number only" on request **[DECIDE]**.
2. Do not photograph patients or screens with patient data on staff phones.

## Staff

1. Each staff member signs a **confidentiality undertaking** at hiring **[DRAFT one, or ask your HR]**.
2. Never share a login. Never read records for curiosity: access is audited.
3. Report a lost phone, a wrong-patient mistake or a suspected leak to the data protection contact the same day (see 05).

## Withdrawal

A patient may ask to withdraw a consent (for example, stop WhatsApp contact). Record it and follow 06.
Withdrawal does not delete medical records that must be kept by law.
