# 06 Requests to see, correct or withdraw (DRAFT)

The PDPA gives people the right to ask to access and to correct their personal data, and to withdraw consent
or limit processing in some cases **[CONFIRM the deadlines, the allowed fee and the grounds to refuse]**.
The 2024 amendment also added a right related to moving data to another provider (portability) **[CONFIRM scope and date]**.

## One owner

The data protection contact receives every request, logs it and tracks the deadline. Staff who receive a request
at the counter pass it on the same day.

## Steps

1. **Receive** the request in writing where possible (a short form at the counter or an email). Record date and who asked.
2. **Check identity.** Match against a government ID in person, or by the number on the record. Never release data to a caller you cannot verify. A guardian may ask for a minor; check their relationship.
3. **Log** it: date received, type (access, correction, withdrawal), who is handling it, deadline **[CONFIRM days]**.
4. **Gather the data** (see below). Do not disclose other people's data. Black out anything about someone else.
5. **Reply** in writing within the deadline, or say why you cannot (for example, the law requires you to keep it, or it would reveal another person's data) and tell them they may complain to the Commissioner **[CONFIRM wording]**.
6. **Record** what was sent or changed and keep the request log **[period, see 04]**.

## How to gather the data from KPOne

| Request | Who can do it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Patient details | CA Supervisor or Director | Patient page; CA Supervisor and Director also see the change history |
| Patient visits and invoices | CA, CA Supervisor | Invoices (visit history) |
| Clinical notes | **Only the treating doctor** can open them. The clinic decides how the doctor produces a copy for the patient (medical-record rules apply) **[DECIDE, CONFIRM]** | Consultation |
| Issued letters | Printed copies from the document history | Visit page |
| Staff file | HR Manager or Director; staff can see their own under My HR details | HR Records |

KPOne has **no "export my data" button**. A request is answered by a person printing or copying the relevant pages.
If volume grows, ask for a reviewed export tool later.

## Correction

- Patient details: CA Supervisor or Director edits the patient (KPOne records the change history).
- A legal name, IC number or date of birth change: check the document first.
- Clinical content is never silently edited. A doctor adds a correction or marks an entry in error; the original stays in the ledger.
- Staff file: a staff member asks HR to correct legal name, IC, date of birth, nationality or bank details; someone else applies it.

## Withdrawal and deletion

- Stop optional uses (for example WhatsApp contact): note it on the patient's record or in the request log **[DECIDE how to flag it, since KPOne has no contact-preference field]**.
- **Deleting a patient record is not offered.** Medical and tax rules usually require you to keep it. Explain this to the requester **[CONFIRM]**.
- For a job applicant: the applicant can delete their own application from their private link (owner, 2026-11-11; it clears the same values as the retention purge, with any talent-pool copy). At the Offered stage, or without the link, HR deletes it on request.
- For a staff member who has left: keep what the law requires, delete the rest after the period in 04.

## Do not

- Do not edit the database by hand.
- Do not charge a fee unless the Act allows it **[CONFIRM]**.
- Do not ignore a request. A late reply is a breach of the rules by itself.
