## Failures

### Security

1. **[SECURITY: scope, needs an owner ruling] branch_manager can see other branches' Insights totals.**
   - Where: /insights/sales?branch=CHERAS, /insights/today and /insights/payments.
   - What happens: picking another branch or "All branches" shows Cheras or organisation-wide totals. These are aggregates only, with no patient data.
   - Why it matters: the 21 Nov decision says Branch Managers get own-branch reports, and the CSV export already refuses other branches (BranchOnlyExportScope). But `insights.view.organisation` has been granted to every role since October.
   - Code: ClinicInsightsController.

No other security issues were found:
- the locum gets 403 on 15 clinical, billing and stock pages;
- the TV gets 403 on patient, queue and Insights pages;
- administrator, technical_admin, business_development and marketing get 403 on 20 patient, visit, billing, dispensary, Panel and report URLs;
- medical_assistant gets 403 on the encounter;
- account_assistant gets 403 on every approval, including a direct POST.

### Blockers

2. **[BLOCKER] Dispensary "Save all lines" saves the same line in a loop after a whole-number quantity edit.**
   - Where: ca, /dispensary/{case}.
   - What happens: changing a saved line's quantity to a whole number such as 12 and pressing Save all lines PUTs the same line 6 times, writing 6 dispensary.item_edited audit rows in 4 seconds. It ends with "This Dispensary record changed". A single Save line leaves the card dirty, and Print label and Complete stay hidden until a reload.
   - Cause: `isDirty` in resources/js/pages/Dispensary/Show.vue compares "12" with "12.000" as strings.
   - Status: the same code is on main. Fixed in #1274 at 6c3691f9.
3. **[BLOCKER, setup] The drug label refuses to print.**
   - Where: ca, /dispensary/{case}/labels.
   - What happens: printing is blocked because `label_address` is empty, so the label falls back to the full address, which is 99 characters on 3 rows against a limit of 96 characters on 2 rows.
   - Fix: set a Label address of 96 characters or fewer, on one line, in Business details for every branch.
4. **[BLOCKER, process] Payments pause while a cash count waits for approval.**
   - Where: ca, /visits/{visit}/billing.
   - What happens: Finalize and Add payment disappear until the count is approved or returned.
   - Why it is easy to hit: approval is possible only after midnight, branch time (CashClosingService.php:220). The banner names a "Branch Manager" (BranchDayGate.php), but ca_supervisor, director and finance_officer can also approve.
   - Before Monday: approve or return every pending count, and tell CAs to count cash only at closing.
5. **[BLOCKER, environment] Clock in/out is refused when PHP has no gd.**
   - What happens: a 422 response, from ClockPhotoFiles.php:52.
   - Status on the Monday server: the owner checked kpjaya.com on 27 Nov and found PHP 8.4.28 with gd present, so this does not affect Monday.
