## 1. Recommendation for the owner

Start with **HR-1: a small roster planner for staff who already have KPOne accounts**, across the three branches. HR drafts, the Director approves, and each worker sees only their own approved shifts. Record planned sessions, breaks and optional proposed rooms; detect overlapping shifts across branches. Do not turn the roster into clock-in evidence, wages, branch access, registration eligibility or a replacement for the doctor's existing room choice. Missing effective branch access is a refusal to approve a shift, not a reason to grant access automatically.

Follow with **HR-2 credential-expiry tracking**, then **HR-3 leave requests and balances**. Introduce reminders inside KPOne, calculated when a permitted screen is opened. Keep payroll calculations, bank details, payslips, statutory submissions and payments in an external payroll package. Later, export only reviewed payroll inputs from KPOne using a separately authorised, accountant-agreed format. No particular external product has been evaluated or recommended.

| Question | Recommended default | Why / risk of the alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Where to begin? | Roster planning only in HR-1; expiry next, leave next. | Immediate coordination value with limited sensitive data. Building attendance, payroll and personnel files together hides policy gaps and broadens access. |
| What is a shift? | Planned start/end instants, explicit branch timezone, breaks, branch and session type; optional proposed room. | A date-only calendar cannot resolve overnight shifts or cross-branch overlaps. A planned shift does not prove hours worked. |
| Who manages it? | Director and HR Manager; Director approves roster changes; own approved roster for human staff. | Giving operational administrators the whole HR area risks personnel disclosure. A future branch-coordinator role needs a separate narrow decision. |
| Does it control clinical work? | No, initially. Separate later integration with warnings before any blocking rule. | An incomplete roster must not unexpectedly stop clinical care or grant access. |
| What about credentials? | Track evidence references and expiry/review dates separately; preserve Director-only verification of professional name/MMC. | An expiry reminder is not regulator verification and must not silently change document issuance. |
| Leave and attendance? | Policy-approved leave ledger first; attendance pilot later, without biometrics or continuous location. | A clocking device cannot settle leave entitlements, overtime approval or pay. |
| What stays external? | Payroll, statutory processing, salary/bank master and sensitive case files initially. | Duplicate calculations and parallel masters create reconciliation and privacy risks. |
| What must be settled before HR-1? | Participants, decision rights, shift rules, branch scope, privacy/retention policy and no clinical gating. | Otherwise the first slice would invent clinic policy. See decision table in section 9. |

No HR build starts from an answer to this report alone: the owner must explicitly authorise the chosen slice, grants and policy boundaries. Statutory items throughout this report are **to confirm with the clinic's accountant, HR adviser or lawyer**, never legal, tax or payroll advice.

## 4. Proposed sensitive-data and access design

### Separate the records, not just the tabs

Keep account identity/access in the existing Identity services. Introduce future HR tables within the modular monolith, with organisation-bound numeric keys and deliberate references to staff profiles. Do not append salary, bank, IC, health or disciplinary fields to `users`, the directory summary or shared Inertia props (F10–F11). An employee/engagement may eventually exist without a login; HR-1 is deliberately limited to existing staff accounts to avoid inventing that lifecycle.

Field-level authenticated encryption is recommended for any later retained salary, bank account, IC, health narrative and disciplinary content; private files need encrypted bytes, integrity hashes, tightly authorised streaming and no public URLs. Do not put sensitive plaintext into search indexes, exception messages, request logs, audit, telemetry, browser persistence or retry payloads. Use keyed payload fingerprints, not guessable hashes of salaries/ICs. Lists should expose only necessary metadata; no decrypt-all search. Encryption does not prevent an operator with both database and application keys from reading data: restrict key custody, backups and operational access separately. No such key/deployment work is authorised here.

“Own” means server-bound subject identity, not a caller-supplied employee id. Own self-service does not let anyone approve their own leave, pay or disciplinary outcome. The no-access entries concern new HR data; they do not remove existing directory/account-management grants. Protected Director records remain protected; default HR may not change the Director's private record. A decision requiring an independent approver for the Director or HR Manager must be settled explicitly; until then that case stays external rather than silently self-approved.

Suggested names are `hr.roster.manage.organisation`, `hr.roster.approve.organisation`, `hr.roster.view.own`; later `hr.credentials.manage.organisation`, `hr.credentials.view.own`, `hr.leave.manage.organisation`, `hr.leave.approve.organisation`, `hr.leave.request.own`, `hr.employment.manage.organisation`, `hr.documents.view.organisation`, and `hr.payroll_inputs.export.organisation`. Each read, evidence view and export needs its own policy check; “manage” must not imply arbitrary export. A branch-coordinator view, if authorised later, would be a separate `hr.roster.view.branch` with names/coverage only, not a department-wide HR grant. No new role is recommended initially.

Keep `canManage`, `canAssignRoles`, `StaffPolicy`, `StaffProvisioningService` and `BranchAssignmentService` rules unchanged. New HR permissions are operational unless the owner expressly decides otherwise; never add them to `AUTHORITY_OVER_PEOPLE_AND_ACCESS` automatically. HR record mutations must also respect existing subject-governance boundaries. Account changes still use the existing services; HR onboarding creates a request, not privileged execution under another actor. Test Director-only provisioning of website-bearing roles unchanged. New permissions may make some other roles no longer assignable by a non-holder: disclose that result, never add grants or weaken a fixture to hide it.

### Audit, retention, export and self-service

Recommend fresh permission/organisation/effective-branch checks on every request and commit, transaction, consistent locks, version checks, UUID retries with payload-conflict refusal and audit-failure rollback for every HR mutation. Private evidence reads/exports should audit before release. Use actor/subject ids, record/version ids, operation, field names and export filters/row count; no salary, bank, IC, medical reason, file body or disciplinary narrative. Existing identity before/after audits (F11) are not a template for sensitive HR values. Restrict HR audit detail independently: existing `audit.view.organisation`, including Technical Admin, must not automatically reveal sensitive HR case metadata or reasons. Any necessary audit-view change needs its own scoped authorisation and tests.

Choose retention per category, purpose and legal hold before collecting it; apply the same policy to files, revisions, exports and backups. Retention periods and PDPA obligations are **to confirm with the clinic's accountant, HR adviser or lawyer**. Do not copy medical-document immutability/no-purge rules wholesale into all HR records. Keep approved work/pay input history correction-oriented, with replacement/adjustment evidence, but design lawful deletion and legal holds explicitly before go-live. No arbitrary “keep forever” default and no purge job proposed for the first slice without an agreed policy.

Exports stay off by default. A later payroll-input CSV should be a deliberate, approved period snapshot, exact integer units/money where relevant, UTF-8 BOM, proper quoting, formula-neutralised text, private/no-store, synchronous with a hard bounded row cap/refusal, throttling, no stored download and values-free audit that must succeed first. Do not export bank/IC or medical evidence to map staff if the external payroll key suffices. Staff self-service starts with own approved roster, then own leave request/balance; salary/bank and legal-name changes require a reviewed correction flow, never self-editing the access profile or payroll master.

Store event instants in UTC; use the branch timezone for displayed dates and local shift/holiday rules. Represent intervals as start inclusive/end exclusive so back-to-back shifts do not overlap. Overnight shifts need explicit start/end dates. Integer minutes are suitable for planned/actual time; leave charging units depend on approved policy, not floating-point fractions guessed from a calendar. Check cross-branch overlaps transactionally. A roster is a plan, attendance is an observation, leave is an entitlement decision, and a payroll input is an approved summary: keep separate records and corrections.

## 6. Reminders without unauthorised messaging

Recommend in-app lists computed on authorised reads: upcoming shifts and changes, uncovered/conflicting sessions, credentials nearing review/expiry, pending leave approvals, probation/contract review tasks later, and offboarding tasks later. Example reminder windows such as 60/30/7 days are **proposed operating settings, not legal deadlines**; the clinic's chosen cadence needs owner confirmation. Show “not recorded” and “expired” separately. Do not assert a reminder means somebody read it.

HR-1 can show the approved roster and conflicts when the user opens its page. Later an authorised own-dashboard count can link to a scoped task list; HR sees operational reminders, a worker sees their own, a coordinator sees only coverage. No diagnoses or medical evidence in badges, browser titles, shared dashboards or waiting-room TV. Acknowledgements, if later required, are separate auditable actions, not proof from a page load. In-app-only reminders are missed when nobody signs in: require a named human checking the list, whose actual identity/procedure **needs owner confirmation**.

Do not use WhatsApp API, automatic click-to-chat, SMS, email, push, webhooks, external calendar or a new queue/job as an assumed channel. WA-01's patient phone click link is not staff messaging permission. No notification implementation is authorised by this analysis. Any future scheduled reminder or messaging delivery needs its own authorisation, purpose, recipient rules and failure handling.
