## 1. Recommendation

Keep KPOne as the clinic's **operational billing and collection source**, with controlled back-office evidence and exports. Keep the general ledger, bank reconciliation, supplier payments, payroll, statutory processing and financial statements in the accounting/payroll packages. Do not build a second accounting system or infer that existing Billing's `FinancialLedger` is a general ledger.

I would **split the owner's proposed first slice** rather than combine hard month-end close, accounting journals and accounts payable. There are three material gaps:

1. Current reports are live projections. Later adjustments and recording reversals restate earlier cohorts, current payment-method classification can restate history, and the Panel report ages current accepted outstanding as of today. A live CSV is not a closed-period snapshot.
2. There is no verified accounting import format, chart-of-accounts mapping, recognition policy or employing/legal-entity structure. These **need owner confirmation** with the accountant before an importable journal can be specified.
3. Purchase orders and goods receipts are not supplier invoices or proof of an unpaid liability. There is no supplier-bill/payment ledger in the inspected purchasing stack, and the broader inventory review is parked.

**Recommended first slice: FIN-A, a reviewed period reconciliation pack and accountant-facing staging CSV.** Finance prepares; a different authorised reviewer, initially the Director, approves. Capture the exact sources, definitions, configuration version, totals, exceptions and generation time. Call it a **review pack**, not a completed month-end close or a posted journal. It does not lock patient operations, create accounts payable or export patient names. Its file layout must be agreed first; if the accountant requires journals immediately, FIN-A cannot proceed on an invented format.

Next: FIN-B, a single-package accounting export adapter after mappings are approved; FIN-C, supplier-bill register/matching view, with payments external; FIN-D, cash handover/banking evidence; then FIN-E, genuine period-close controls after late-change and collection rules are settled. Refunds and provider/doctor payments remain separate decisions, never incidental adjustments to existing services. Prioritise an urgent cash-control gap ahead of FIN-C if the owner confirms one; the current procedure **needs owner confirmation**.

## 5. Definitions the accountant must agree before export

### Do not combine date bases or measures

- **Invoice net sales:** finalised, non-void invoice cohort by branch-local finalisation date, adjusted payable. Gross invoice-line values plus surcharge minus discount reconcile to that amount. Original line rankings are not net per-service income (F2–F4).
- **Patient receipts:** whole receipts by original receipt date, original/reversed/net columns; later recording reversals currently restate that original date (F5/F10). Allocation-based Payments cards can differ from whole receipt totals where unallocated evidence exists; do not silently substitute the card for the shared receipt projection (`docs/INSIGHTS.md:137–161`).
- **Panel responsibility:** a settlement responsibility, not money. **Claim acceptance/remittance:** different measures and a separate flow from patient receipts. Remittance date is its recorded date, not a bank-settlement timestamp (F11).
- **Due now, approved pay-later remaining and Panel accepted outstanding:** distinct states. Daily summary “Due” is approved pay-later remaining in an invoice-finalisation cohort; “Total received” excludes Panel and Due. Do not add all of them as one daily revenue total (`DailySummaryReportService.php:48–60`; `docs/reports/RPT-01c.md:9–13`).
- **Purchase commitment, goods received and supplier bill:** different events. Stock unit cost may be estimated/fallback, and quantities-only stock reports do not establish inventory valuation or cost of sales.

The operational ledger's identity is `adjusted payable = net patient allocations + approved Panel responsibility + approved pay-later remaining + due now`. It is not a general-ledger journal. Accountant-approved recognition, clearing/control accounts, debit/credit mapping and tax treatment **need owner confirmation**; this report does not prescribe booking entries.

### Snapshot time is not a past-date reconstruction

FIN-A should label a period pack “period cohort, state captured at [UTC/local time]”, with immutable source/configuration versions once approved. A receipt, claim decision or reversal recorded after the nominal month end cannot silently be presented as known at that earlier date. The current Panel summary's invoice-date selection and today's ageing cannot be summed into a truthful all-history past-month receivable balance. A whole receivables position must include older unpaid invoices/claims and distinguish their statuses; use a separately defined projection, not just the last 31 dates.

Recommendation: FIN-A freezes what was reviewed at preparation time and records exceptions; it makes **no retrospective as-of accounting claim**. FIN-B introduces accountant-approved posting dates and later-change/delta handling. Closed-period event treatment must be decided before FIN-E. Preserve original business date, recorded time, accounting posting date and imported period as distinct fields. A correction recorded in an open period must link its old source without changing the frozen prior export or existing RPT date semantics.

### Refund policy to settle explicitly

Recommend a real refund process distinguish: (1) an erroneous receipt record; (2) a valid collection returned to a patient; (3) an invoice adjustment/credit obligation; (4) provider chargeback; and (5) cancellation of a prepayment. Only case (1) uses today's recording-error reversal and its existing restrictions. A valid refund needs original-source link, entitlement/credit decision, capped amount net of prior refunds, approved reason, independent approver, execution evidence/date/method and accounting acknowledgement. A recorded approval is not proof money was returned. Partial refunds, completed visits, returned stock, Panel-paid invoices and third-party payers need separate rules. No automatic stock return, invoice void, recreated debt or Panel change. Actual current refund procedure **needs owner confirmation**; legal/tax document treatment is **to confirm with the clinic's accountant or tax adviser**.

### Doctor fees and commissions cannot use an unstated percentage

Ask for each agreement: fixed session versus percentage/mixed; gross or net; earned versus collected; consultation/procedure/medicine exclusions; handling of invoice discounts, surcharge, Panel acceptance/shortfall, bad debt/refund and split providers; rate effective date, rounding, minimum/cap, self-review and pay approval. Actual clinic rules **need owner confirmation**. Prefer a worked, fictional example signed off before any code. Current checkout doctor is a source reference, not proof that all revenue belongs to that doctor or that a later roster change should restate pay. If a service was CA-added, its billing existence does not establish a doctor fee entitlement.

Default until agreed: calculations external, no automatic payout. A later engine freezes a versioned rule and source set, exact sen/units, per-line rounding policy and exception list; it produces a proposal with independent approval, not a bank instruction or wage. Do not allocate discounts proportionally or count rejected Panel amounts by invention. Tax/employment treatment is **to confirm with the clinic's accountant or tax adviser**.

## 6. Controls and export design recommendations

**Separation of duties:** Finance prepares packs/mappings/bills, a different Director approves; importer acknowledges actual acceptance in the accounting software. Nobody approves their own refund, supplier bill payment or compensation proposal. If the Director is the claimant/payee or sole preparer, keep the exceptional case external until the owner identifies an independent authorised reviewer; do not silently self-approve. Preserve existing exceptions exactly: BP-01's same-actor responsibility approval within configured limits remains; PO creator independence, recording-reversal independence and adjustment-reversal independence remain. No single generic “Finance admin” grant.

**Access:** propose new narrow `.branch` view/prepare/export permissions for operational packs, with separate `.organisation` mapping and approval permissions where cross-branch accounting really requires them. Default Finance Officer and Director only; Director approves, Finance prepares. Each branch dataset requires explicit effective-branch authority; an organisation export must either validate assignments to every included branch or have a separately approved organisation-wide contract. Foreign branches always refused. Existing Administrator aggregate permissions do not authorise accounting batch, AP, bank, patient or compensation rows. Panel Officer retains existing claim duties and supplies identifiers/status, not new general ledger or payroll access. Technical Admin, Marketing, BD and HR gain nothing by implication.

**Mutations:** fresh permission/organisation/effective-branch and subject checks, DB transaction, stable lock order, independent lock_version, UUID retry key with payload-conflict refusal, and audit failure rollback. Limit tables are explicit, effective-dated and cannot be self-raised. Keep approved snapshots/actions append-oriented, database-enforced against update/delete/truncate where appropriate. Do not delete financial sources or relax existing PostgreSQL guards to implement a close. Existing audits are not all values-free (terminal totals and some claim amounts are logged, F9/F11); new workflow/export audits should use ids, versions, field names, state, scope and counts, never rows, patient names, notes, reasons, account numbers or documents. Sensitive reasons stay in protected workflow evidence, not broad logs.

**Export approach:** one reviewed adapter for one confirmed package/version/import format; accountant supplies chart-of-accounts, branch/cost-centre and counterparty mappings plus test cases. Version mappings separately from current payment classifications, so an Administrator changing Cash/Non-cash cannot change an approved export. Block unmapped or ambiguous rows rather than guess a default account. Use stable source key/version plus event kind, batch number/hash, currency and period; the package's returned journal/import reference is separate evidence. Do not send patient names, NRIC, diagnosis, medicines, Bill to, notes or adjustment reasons unless a separately approved requirement proves necessity. Aggregate by approved account/branch/date where sufficient, keeping internal source links for authorised reconciliation.

For an importable journal, require exact integer-sen balance within each accountant-defined journal, documented rounding, no silent balancing plug, and a source-to-batch reconciliation. Dedupe at both source/version and import batch. Downloading twice is allowed but clearly labelled as the **same batch**, not another posting. “Downloaded” and “imported” are different statuses; record operator-confirmed import id/date, never claim a browser download proves a posting. A rejected import does not mutate billing; corrected mappings create a new review version with explicit supersession/delta and handling of anything already imported. No direct API, bank credential, job or network call initially.

Reuse the CSV safety and throttle mechanisms, not necessarily the human-report header layout: accounting importers may reject BOM/metadata rows or interpret cells differently. Recommended first format is a human-review BOM/CRLF CSV using ReportCsv safety; FIN-B needs an explicit importer compatibility contract and tests before a different machine CSV is authorised. Never weaken formula neutralisation just to make an importer accept a dangerous field. Hard cap/refusal, no partial export, no-store and pre-release audit; partition oversized output into deterministic approved batches rather than truncate. Existing RPT contracts/caps stay unchanged. Keep generated downloads ephemeral; retaining a close snapshot/batch manifest is a **new storage authorisation**, not permission to store today's CSVs or a ZIP of patient data.

**Period controls:** distinguish review-pack approval, export freeze, imported accounting period and hard operational lock. FIN-A/B must not claim to block billing writers. A later hard lock needs coverage of every relevant invoice/adjustment/receipt/reversal/claim/remittance/bill mutation and concurrent close/write race. Do not freeze an old invoice so completely that a legitimate later debt receipt cannot be posted in the open period. Lock the agreed posting events, not every unrelated patient or clinical operation. Reopening requires independent authority, reason and version; never overwrite the original approved/imported pack. No statutory date or retention rule is assumed: **to confirm with the clinic's accountant or tax adviser**.

**External boundaries:** the accounting package owns its chart, final books, bank reconciliation and supplier payments. KPOne keeps its operational source identifiers and the accepted transfer evidence. A spreadsheet can prove an early mapping or fee example, but it must have a reviewed owner/version and not become a competing mutable master. Recovery/restore, key custody and access review must be proven before real data. Any retained bank details, supplier documents, compensation evidence or patient data needs separately scoped privacy and encryption decisions; none is collected by this analysis.
